A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Ancient Wisdom.
A More Aligned You.

I'm Aartee Bajaj, an Astro-Vastu Consultant, Vedic Astrologer, Life Coach and Yoga Teacher based in Dubai.

My journey into holistic wellbeing began over a decade ago, driven by a deep curiosity about how our minds, our surroundings and our personal experiences influence the way we live.

Today, through Keep It Together, I work with individuals and families who are seeking greater clarity, balance and a more conscious way of living.

For me, this work is not about predicting or controlling every outcome. It's about understanding ourselves better, recognising patterns and making more informed choices.

I believe that every person, every home and every journey is unique.
